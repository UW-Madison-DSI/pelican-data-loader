About this demo

A minimum viable dataset repository for UW–Madison. Datasets are stored in UW–Madison Research Object S3, described with Croissant metadata, served over the Pelican Platform, and indexed in an internal PostgreSQL database.

Discover

For end users. Search and filter published datasets, then copy a snippet that loads one over Pelican with Hugging Face datasets.

Publish

For library data stewards. Describe a CSV, upload it, generate and validate Croissant metadata, and record the dataset.
