{# The canonical UW Theme footer. The UW Theme ships exactly one footer pattern -- charcoal #282728, #adadad body text, four columns, copyright row -- and the design system is explicit that you don't fork it: if a page needs the footer to feel lighter or darker, you change the page above it instead. Rebuilt here with Tailwind/daisyUI utilities rather than uw-style.css selectors, since this app doesn't load the official stylesheet. Structure and content follow ui_kits/web/footer.html from the design system. The Quick Links block is the set UW expects on university pages -- leave it intact. Colours are literal rather than themed: this footer is charcoal on every UW page regardless of the surrounding palette. #}
